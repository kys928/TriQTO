## Blind metadata / cryptographic custody

**Threat model:** adversary has the public repository, generator algorithms, fixed task contract, every public root ID, clean circuit, ordered blind observation IDs, shot metadata and archive. Adversary can execute any public deterministic PRNG or inspect known sample conventions. Encryption of a truth file alone does not protect a deterministic public label schedule.

- An isolated trusted acquisition process draws a secret-keyed, uniformly random 13-position permutation per root (e.g. secure per-root HMAC/HKDF-derived seed from secret master key, or CSPRNG random permutation). **Do not seed it from public ID, public sample ordinal or public RNG namespace.**
- Generate observation IDs as cryptographically random 128-bit+ opaque identifiers, independently of the truth class, strength/shot choice and secret schedule. Do not use sorted IDs as implicit labels. Keep acquisition order internally randomized and avoid timestamp/filename/byte-size metadata that reconstructs the original labels.
- Diagnostic shot level still correlates with strength bin under the frozen task; therefore disclose shot count for valid inference but **do not claim strength-bin secrecy based only on encrypted truth**. All three mechanisms at a fixed bin share shots, so shot level alone need not reveal mechanism. Treat possible statistical mechanism inference from measured counts as ordinary learnable signal, not metadata leakage.
- Seal mechanism/strength/bin, clean-control indicator, injection location, secret permutation and full generator recipes under custodian-held authenticated encryption; never put any permutation seed or reconstructable deterministic schedule in public manifests or published source. Publish only opaque rows and observation-validity metadata required by the model. Clean controls need a predeclared scoring role and must not leak their position.
- Provide blinded scoring through a trusted evaluator that accepts committed SHA256 predictions and returns only predeclared aggregate metrics and family-cluster CI. Keep test truth custody outside the authoring/testing workflow; no untrusted PR runner may read decryption keys.
- Before release: attack the blind records alone with tests for deterministic shuffle recovery, observation ID class correlations, counts-size leakage, strength/shot label leakage, and generator-seed reproducibility. Verify against *permitted synthetic/FIT mock truth*, not future TEST outcomes.

## Location-proxy limitation

The frozen Step-14 family blueprint places its **first RY on the intended affected qubit**, and its legal boundary is deterministic given a family injection context and the entangling-event positions. Publishing the context class thus reveals the location without opening sealed labels. Do not expose injection context in a TEST inference manifest. However, the circuit's first RY proxy remains even when context is suppressed. **Preserving the exact original grammar makes strict structural qubit-location secrecy impossible.** Call it a known location proxy and bound scientific claims accordingly; do not falsely claim location-agnostic tests. A different location-neutral circuit grammar is an explicitly separate future scientific question, not an implicit amendment to this finite-shot stabilization study.

## Independent scoring boundaries

- TRAIN/FIT and SELECTION on an eligible, attested separate development cohort only, final frozen remedy selected once using paired instability and mechanism accuracy noninferiority. Controls are analyzed separately; clean observations must not be folded into the three-class mechanism metric.
- Blind TEST, once, after SHA256 frozen candidate code/settings/feature contract/pooling, baseline comparisons, no method updates, no post-test sample selection or threshold adjustments.
- Repeated calibration draws are not independent families. Use family-clustered paired uncertainty for stability and mechanism accuracy; controls and distorted rows are distinct. Report N primary, 2N/4N secondary, full propagated pooling and independent score-path-only analyses.
- Even with correct encryption, visible diagnostic *measurement counts* may allow inferential classification. That is the target signal; leakage means deterministic access to label truth from **metadata alone**.
